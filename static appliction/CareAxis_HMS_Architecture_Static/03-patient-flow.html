Patient journey and department hand-offs

OPD is shown as the common outpatient path, with emergency and inpatient branches for patients who need further care.

Outpatient (OPD) journey

01 · Arrival / appointment check-inConfirm booking or start walk-in registration
↓
02 · Patient registrationVerify identity and UHID
↓
03 · Triage / vitals / queueCapture initial observations and token
↓
04 · Doctor consultationReview history, document findings and diagnosis
↓
05 · DiagnosticsOrder lab tests or imaging and track results
↓
06 · Clinical reviewReview results and prescribe treatment
↓
07 · Pharmacy and billingDispense medicines and settle charges
↓
08 · Home / follow-upShare reports and schedule follow-up
↓

Emergency and inpatient branch

01 · Emergency triageImmediate clinical prioritisation; do not delay urgent care for billing
↓
02 · StabilisationRecord interventions and escalation
↓
03 · Admission decisionConsultant decision, payer context and bed request
↓
04 · Bed and ward assignmentAssign location and start inpatient record
↓
05 · Ward careVitals, medication administration, rounds and handover
↓
06 · Procedure / OT when requiredSchedule, consent, safety checks and recovery
↓
07 · Discharge readinessSummary, medication, final bill and follow-up
